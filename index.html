A and D run, W climbs, left click jumps and right click yells. Hold E beside a supply crate to take hold of it: walk and it comes with you either way, so a crate shoved into a wall is never the end of the puzzle. The pressure plates catch a crate as it passes and hold it there, and once it has clicked in you can lean on it all you like without knocking it off — grab it and haul it out again. Words do not shift a crate: only your own hands do. Prefer two hands on the keyboard? Arrow keys move and climb, . jumps, / yells and , grabs. (Space and X still work too.) On a phone, tap Touch controls for a four-way pad. The last bell has gone, your hall pass is a forgery, and the exit is always upstairs. One hit does not end you. You walk in wearing a hat and a backpack, so you can see your own health on your own back: the first hit knocks the hat off, the second takes the pack, the third takes the life. Find a phone and it rides in your hand as a fourth hit — and while you are filming, the hype comes in twice as fast and your yell recharges quicker. Every floor of every period has a checkpoint, so losing a life sends you back to the landing you reached, not to the front door. Each period also has its own track, from the yard's opening riff to the hush of the library to whatever is going on in that clock tower. You always have 67, but there are seven more words hidden across the eight periods and every one of them fires differently: SKIBIDI fans out three ways, RIZZ is a beam down the whole corridor, TUNG TUNG TUNG barely needs recharging, OHIO finds them for you, FANUM TAX comes back and brings the stars with it, AURA goes off in every direction and CRASH OUT clears the screen. They run out, and you lose whatever you were holding when you die. Keep landing yells without getting hit and the hype meter fills — at the top the whole school starts chanting, and for seven seconds nothing can touch you. Mind the library: it is a quiet zone, every word you throw in there is heard, and the Librarians cannot be shouted down, stomped or beaten: when they come, you run and jump clear of them. And mind the pink hallway, where your whole arsenal is worthless: every word you throw at the Plastics fizzles, because no one says that anymore. What does land is their own line. Wait for ON WEDNESDAYS WE WEAR PINK to reach you, yell at that exact moment, and you catch it and send it straight back — and their words do work on them. Miss, and your yell is still recharging when it hits you. Each period is graded A to F on time, stars and hits taken. One more thing: yelling the plain 67 too much strains your voice. A bar fills over your head, and when it tops out you go hoarse — you cannot yell at all for a couple of seconds, until the bar on YELL refills. Pick up a word and you can yell without limit. Every period hides a secret locker — yell at the one that is rattling for a free life. Grab every star for a bonus, beat the par time, and earn trophies; the title screen has a Speedrun mode, a records page, and options for sound, haptics and an assist mode. Gamepads work too. Press H (or the house button on a phone) to quit to the main menu at any time — where you got to is saved, and CONTINUE picks it back up.
